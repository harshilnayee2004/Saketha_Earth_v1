## 1. Executive Concept

This redesign transforms **Saketha Earth** into a world-class architectural benchmark for ultra-luxury living and private landholdings, directly inspired by high-editorial architectural monographs (Aman, Foster + Partners, and Architectural Digest).

It integrates the two core visual directions requested by the client:
1. **3D Text-Behind-Building Hero with Scroll Emergence (Reference Image 2)**:
   - **Initial State**: Monumental typography (*"Where Luxury Finds Its Home"*) sits behind the villa facade and roofline at `z-index: 10`.
   - **Scroll Emergence Engine**: As the user turns the scroll wheel, the text rises up and smoothly transitions to the foreground at `z-index: 25`, completely unblocked with every single letter 100% visible and sharp.
   - Cursor-driven spring parallax creates authentic 3D spatial depth.
   - Features the floating frosted pearl glass pill CTA: `» Find Your Luxury`.
2. **Light Premium Editorial Bento Grid (Reference Image 1)**:
   - **Canvas & Surfaces**: Light, warm alabaster, porcelain, and travertine cards (`#FAF8F5`, `#FFFFFF`) with crisp charcoal typography (`#14161B`) and fine champagne hairlines (`#EAE4D9`).
   - **Card 1 — "Represent the extraordinary"**: Aerial photography of private estate enclaves with concise, refined editorial copy and advisor direct link.
   - **Card 2 — "First Floor Plan" CAD Blueprint**: Interactive architectural CAD floor plan with room hotspots (Primary Suite, Great Room, Kitchen, Covered Terrace, 3-Car Garage) and live dimension inspector.
   - **Card 3 — "East End Manor" Property Card**: High-end facade photography, architectural specs (4 bd • 4 ba • 480 m²), and prominent price indicator ($5.3M / ₹4.2 Cr).
   - **Card 4 — Cadastral GPS Coordinates Map**: Stylized light vector map with active plot pins ($3.5M, $5.3M, $7.2M) and DTCP verification status.
3. **Animate UI Spring Motion & Micro-Interactions**:
   - Spring physics on cards, buttons, and popups (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
   - Dynamic room inspector with spring scale feedback.
   - Magnetic button pull effect on CTAs.
4. **Restraint & Editorial Clarity**:
   - Removed all redundant, verbose marketing boilerplate ("useless text") to allow typography, whitespace, and architectural photography to take center stage.
